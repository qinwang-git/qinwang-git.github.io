I am a Senior Research Scientist at CSIRO Technology (rebranded from Data61), Australia. I completed my PhD at Swinburne University of Technology. I received my Master’s degree from Beihang University (BUAA) and my Bachelor’s degree from Northwestern Polytechnical University (NPU).

My additional/honorary appointments and roles include:
- adjunct senior lecturer at UNSW Sydney and University of Technology Sydney (UTS)
- affiliated researcher at Digital Finance CRC (DFCRC)
- senior member of IEEE

My research centers on trustworthy distributed computation—how to make large-scale decentralized systems secure, privacy-preserving, and incentive-compatible. My work builds on foundations in applied cryptography, distributed systems, and network measurement.

- Layer2 Protocols (e.g, DAO, governance)
- Layer1 Protocols (e.g, consensus, incentives)
- agentic payments

Distributed computation

- distributed learning (e.g., split/federated/unlearning)
